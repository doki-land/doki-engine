## Themes own the surface

A DejaVu theme controls the visual grammar of a site: layout, typography, navigation, and the small details that make a publication feel authored. Your Markdown and structured data stay in the content folder.

## Plugins add behavior

Plugins can add search, feeds, image processing, analytics, or project-specific hooks. They are installed capabilities, not a hosted dashboard that owns your source files.

```toml
[plugins]
enabled = ["search", "feeds"]
```

Start with a small theme, then add only the capabilities your publication actually needs.
